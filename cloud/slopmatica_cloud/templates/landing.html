Your schematics, on every machine.

Slopmatica Cloud keeps your schematics (.slopmatic, vanilla .nbt, .litematic and WorldEdit .schem) in sync across every game instance logged in with your Minecraft account.

Log in from the game

Open the Slopmatica menu (M) → Cloud → Open website.

There are no accounts to create: the game proves who you are through Mojang, and hands your browser a one-time login link.

Or log in with a password

No password yet? Set one first: open the website from the game (Cloud → Open website), then Account → Set password.

Looking for builds? Browse the schematics board, no login needed.
