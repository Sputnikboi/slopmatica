Made by Sputniknull + Claude

Slopmatica is a schematic mod for Minecraft (Fabric). It shows a build as a hologram of what is missing, correct or wrong, and reads its own .slopmatic files, vanilla .nbt structures and Litematica .litematic files. This site keeps your schematics in sync between your game installs and hosts the schematics board.
